Instructions

  1. 1
    Accept the email invite and set up your DigitalOcean account
  2. 2
    Connect Grok Bot to DigitalOcean

    Go to Connect Apps > DigitalOcean. It installs the DigitalOcean plugin, which comes in seven parts: Droplets, Apps, Accounts, Databases, Networking, Insights and Docs.

  3. 3
    Sign in to each DigitalOcean plugin part from the chat

    Tell the bot: “Sign me in to all the DigitalOcean parts that still need it, one at a time.” Approve each sign-in card that appears. No tokens are needed, and the bot never sees your password.

  4. 4
    Check that Grok Bot sees your account

    Ask “list my droplets” or “what apps do I have?” If it answers, you’re set. The bot asks before changing or deleting anything.

  5. 5
    Add doctl for what the plugin doesn’t coveroptional

    doctl is the DigitalOcean command-line tool. Ask the bot to “install doctl on your computer and sign it in through the browser with read and write access”. It goes on the bot’s own computer, and no token is needed.

  6. 6
    Give the bot Spaces and model access keysoptional

    Spaces uses separate S3-style keys, and the model access key is for calling models. After step 5, ask the bot to “create a full-access Spaces key and a model access key, and save them privately”. The bot never shows the secrets, and you can revoke the keys anytime in the control panel.

  7. 7
    Build and have fun, and we’ll help if you get stuck
